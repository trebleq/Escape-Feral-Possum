How To Play

Keyboard & Mouse

  • WASD — Move
  • Mouse — Look
  • Left Click — Shoot
  • R — Reload
  • Space — Jump
  • Shift — Sprint
  • Ctrl — Crouch
  • 1 / 2 / 3 — Switch Weapon
  • E — Interact
  • M — Map
  • Esc — Pause

Touch

  • Left stick — Move
  • Drag right side — Look
  • Buttons — Shoot / Reload / Jump / Switch / Map

Controller

  • Left Stick — Move
  • Right Stick — Look
  • RT — Shoot, X/Square — Reload
  • Y/Triangle — Switch, B/Circle — Interact
  • Start — Pause, Select — Map

Find keys to open locked doors. Explore for secrets, ammo and health. Standing still gets you killed — strafe, dodge, and keep moving.
